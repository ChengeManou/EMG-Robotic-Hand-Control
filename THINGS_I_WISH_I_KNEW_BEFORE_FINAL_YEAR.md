# Things I Wish Someone Had Told Me Before Starting My Final-Year Project 🎓🤖

*Advice from someone who built a robotic hand and somehow survived the experience.*

- **Make it fun, for goodness' sake.** 😂 You're going to spend months on this thing. You might as well enjoy it. Add something stupid, funny or completely unnecessary if it makes you happy. My robotic hand can do rock'n'roll. Was it essential? Absolutely not. Do I regret it? Absolutely not. 🤘
- **Start early.** ⏰ You will still finish late, but at least you'll have a head start on the suffering.
- **Research is like shopping for a T-shirt.** 👕 Know what you're looking for. Otherwise, you'll spend six hours reading papers and somehow learn nothing useful.
- **Your first results will probably be crap.** 💩 Congratulations, you're doing research!
- **Just because your code runs doesn't mean it works.** 💻 Sometimes your computer is confidently producing complete nonsense.
- **Order your components early.** 📦 Apparently, delivery companies don't care about your academic success.
- **Test things before they're perfect.** 🧪 Otherwise, you'll discover three days before submission that your beautiful design doesn't actually work.
- **Keep a record of everything.** 📝 Especially the stupid mistakes. Those are usually the interesting ones.
- **Listen to your supervisor, but use your own brain.** 🧠 They have experience. You have the responsibility of making the thing work.
- **Don't marry your first design.** 💔 Sometimes the best engineering decision is admitting that your original idea was terrible.
- **Make friends with other students.** 🤝 Shared suffering is surprisingly good for morale.
- **Take breaks and have a life.** 🌴 Your project is important, but you're still a human being, not a poorly cooled microcontroller.
- **Know when to stop improving things.** 🛑 At some point, you have to stop engineering and actually submit the damn report.
- **Be proud of the little victories.** 🎉 When something finally works after three days of debugging, celebrate it. Nobody else will understand why a moving finger is such a big deal.
- **Remember why you chose engineering.** 🔧 Hopefully, at some point, you thought building cool things sounded fun. Don't let deadlines completely ruin that.

---

**Your final-year project is probably going to be frustrating, exhausting, confusing, and occasionally ridiculous. But if you do it right, you might also have a hell of a good time.** 🚀
